# Disclaimer

**AURORA is a research instrument, not a medical device.**

- It makes **no claim** that any frequency treats, cures, prevents or diagnoses any condition.
- It produces **no medical, psychological or therapeutic advice**.
- Its "exploratory score" measures mathematical and historical *interest*, never efficacy.
- Nothing in this repository should be read as evidence that a specific frequency does anything
  to the human body. Testing that question is the purpose of the project; the question is open.

If you have a medical concern, consult a qualified professional. Do not delay or replace
medical care because of anything produced by this software.

## Hearing safety

Output amplitude is capped at 0.2 in code, but your device volume is not. Listen at a
comfortable level. Prolonged exposure to loud sound damages hearing permanently. Stop if a
sound is uncomfortable.

Frequencies outside 20–20,000 Hz can be generated for analysis. They are labelled as
inaudible. Do not attempt to amplify them through equipment designed for audible sound.

## Data

All data stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. If you choose to share an
exported file, you decide what it contains — review it before sending. Exports are plain,
readable JSON for exactly this reason.
