# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue with evidence, not with people.

## Specifics for this project

**Disagreement about the science is welcome and expected.** If you think the plate model is
wrong, the statistics are naive or a corpus citation is bogus — say so directly. Bring your
reasoning. That is the entire point of the project.

**Disagreement about people is not.** Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments,
and unwelcome attention are unacceptable and will get you removed.

**Do not push claims you cannot support.** This project exists because "528 Hz repairs DNA"
circulated for years without evidence. Making the same kind of claim here — in either
direction — misses the point. "I believe X" is fine. "X is proven" needs a source.

**Respect other people's data.** If someone shares their experiment records, treat them as
what they are: something a person chose to give. Do not attempt to identify contributors, and
do not use shared data for anything beyond this research.

**Do not give medical advice.** Not in issues, not in discussions, not anywhere. If someone
describes a health problem, point them to a professional.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere the project is represented.

## Enforcement

Report through a private issue or by contacting the maintainers via GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
